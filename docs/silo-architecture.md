## The Four Categories (silos)

### CATEGORY 1 — THE ADAPTIVE SYSTEM  (NEW · product-wedge cornerstone)
**Why it's a category:** This is the product's actual competitive differentiator — the
"how the program adapts to you" wedge (readiness, autoregulation, RPE, training max vs
e1RM, day-to-day load decisions). It is what separates CMS from a spreadsheet 5/3/1, and
it fits NO existing live category. It is also the language a serious/masters lifter types
when their fixed program stops working — CMS's buyer trigger. Highest product fit,
authority-building, and long-tail potential. Featured "START HERE" candidate once it has
3+ posts (see Decisions).

- **CORNERSTONE (NEEDS: new 2,000+):** `what-is-adaptive-powerlifting-programming` —
  "What Is Adaptive Powerlifting Programming?" Anchor of the category.
- **Spokes (NEEDS: all new):** autoregulation · rpe-for-powerlifting · readiness-scores ·
  training-max-vs-1rm-vs-e1rm · bad-day-workout-adjustments · deload-vs-one-day-adjustment ·
  high-low-stress-days · trends-vs-single-bad-workouts.
- **Engine tie:** Readiness (±5% daily), Load, RPE, CLM. **CTA:** app trial — "let the
  program adapt to you."

## Internal-link + CTA rules (apply to every post, all 4 categories)

1. Spokes link UP to their category cornerstone; cornerstone links DOWN to spokes. One hop.
2. Every post links to the relevant engine section (`/index#engines` or `/index#programs`)
   and ends with an app CTA → `https://app.cmstrength.fit/signup`.
3. Cross-category links only where genuinely topical (e.g., meet-week fueling ↔
   Programming's meet prep; joint-load ↔ Adaptive). Do not force it.
4. Authority-only outbound (PubMed/.gov/.edu). No sibling-product links, ever.
5. Each cornerstone targets its own head keyword; spokes target distinct long-tail
   modifiers to avoid cannibalization (no two posts in a category compete for the same head term).
