The Bar Doesn't Care How You Slept

You woke up at 3:17, stared at the ceiling, and now you're standing in front of the bar with a plan written for a different man. The bar doesn't care how you slept — but today's plan should.

The same 80% that moved like a warm-up on Tuesday can sit like a max on Thursday, and you won't know which until the bar is in your hands. This scorecard takes that guess off the table in under a minute.

Why Readiness Is the Whole Game After 40

At 25 you could ignore the signals and the work still got done. After 40, recovery stops being predictable. Sleep debt, work stress, a hard block, a nagging shoulder: the same program on two different days produces a PR or a setback.

Run the same plan with a 25-year-old and a 46-year-old. One recovers like it's a job. The other's recovery is a weather system, and the forecast changes daily. The young one can ignore all of it and still hit the numbers. You can't, and pretending otherwise is how promising blocks turn into physio appointments.

The cost of guessing wrong isn't one bad session. It's a deadlift you had no business taking, a week of grinding on a stale nervous system, a shoulder that quietly stops the block. Readiness is the readiness after 50 problem: the input that decides whether today's intensity builds you or buries you.

What the Number Means

Five is a body that needs the day off. Twenty-five is a body ready to hunt. Everything between is a dial, not a verdict. Here's the coaching call for each range:

21–25 TRAIN AS PRESCRIBED Run the top sets. The plan was written for today's you.
17–20 KEEP THE SESSION, DROP LOADS ~5% Same work, slightly less steel. The stimulus still lands.
12–16 SUBMAXIMAL / TECHNIQUE DAY 50–65%, crisp reps, zero grinders. Recover without detraining.
5–11 REST OR MOBILITY Walk, mobilize, eat, sleep. Tomorrow is a training day.

What the Number Is Not

It's not a personality test, it's not permission to skip the gym, and it's not a substitute for a warm-up. It's a load adjustment. A low score doesn't mean you're weak. It means the tissue isn't recovered enough to make today's prescribed weight productive, and forcing it buys a setback at full price.
