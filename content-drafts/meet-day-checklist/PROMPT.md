Create a wide 16:9 promotional graphic for a powerlifting gym called Center Mass Strength, styled like a premium fitness advertisement with a gritty black, charcoal, white, and deep red color palette.

Overall composition: Split the image roughly 40/60. The left 40% is a clean black graphic panel containing the headline, short copy, packing checklist icons, and gym branding. The right 60% is a realistic cinematic gym scene showing a female powerlifter packing her meet-day gear.

Right side photo scene: A strong athletic woman in her late 20s to early 30s kneels on the rubber floor of a serious powerlifting gym, packing a large black gym duffel bag. She wears a fitted black Center Mass Strength T-shirt with a simple white-and-red gym logo. Her expression is focused and practical, looking down into the bag rather than at the camera. Hair tied in a messy bun. Behind her are black power racks, barbells, and large red competition-style weight plates. The gym should feel authentic, industrial, dark, and slightly gritty, with soft daylight entering from high windows. Cinematic but believable lighting, shallow depth of field in the background, crisp detail on the athlete and equipment.

- red-and-black powerlifting singlet

- black-and-red wrist wraps

- black lifting shoes
- chalk container
- water bottle / shaker bottle
- packaged snacks such as granola bites and an energy bar
- photo ID / federation membership card
- small personal items such as lip balm or earbuds

Use coordinated black-and-red equipment so the entire scene feels intentionally branded. The items should look realistic, correctly proportioned, and clearly recognizable. Avoid duplicate gear or malformed objects.
