# Internal Linking Rules

## Required topology

- Build a **grid / basket-weave**, not a vertical-only tree.
- Every content cluster needs both:
  - **Vertical links:** post ↔ category hub/index or service pillar.
  - **Lateral links:** post/page ↔ topically related siblings in the same category.

## Per-page rule

- Every post should carry **1–2 lateral links** to same-category sister posts when the relationship is genuinely useful.
- Add links only where a reader would naturally want the next explanation, example, comparison, or deeper guide.
- Never force a relationship, create a link farm, or add obnoxious link density.

## Anchor text

- Write anchors as natural, descriptive phrases woven into the sentence.
- Aim for **at least about 40 characters** so the destination and reason to click are clear.
- Never use generic anchors such as **“read more,” “this post,” “click here,”** or a bare keyword.

## No-orphan gate

- Every indexable page must have **at least one contextual inbound internal link beyond its hub/index listing**.
- Before publishing a new page, identify its hub link, at least one sibling page that will link in, and its 1–2 lateral outbound links.
- Do not mark the page complete until those links exist and resolve.
